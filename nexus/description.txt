[size=5][b]PetPerks[/b][/size]

Each pet gets a perk: cats scare crows, dogs herd farm animals in and out, and turtles boost fish pond roe.

[b]Features[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Cats[/b] scare away crows, so you don't need scarecrows.
[*][b]Dogs[/b] open the barn and coop doors in the morning, let the animals out, herd them back in at night and close the doors.
[*][b]Turtles[/b] make fish ponds produce 1.5x more roe.
[*]Everything is configurable with Generic Mod Config Menu.
[/list]

[list=1]
[*]Install SMAPI.
[*]Download and unzip [i]PetPerks1.0.0.zip[/i] into [i]C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\SteamApps\common\Stardew Valley\Mods\[/i]
[*]Run the game through SMAPI.
[/list]
